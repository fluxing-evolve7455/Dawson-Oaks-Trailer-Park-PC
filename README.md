## 🕹️ About

Dawson Oaks Trailer Park is a multiplayer social-deduction game developed and published by Striped Panda Studios. Set in a colorful trailer park, it places criminal residents, an openly investigating Supervisor, and a hidden Snitch in the same chaotic community. Suspects must complete role-specific criminal urges to maintain their sanity while hiding suspicious behavior through proximity voice chat. Meetings, accusations, secret reports, arrests, and shifting alliances make every round a tense comedy of deception.

Dawson Oaks Trailer Park is built for groups who enjoy social deduction, improvised roleplay, proximity chat, and absurd multiplayer deception.

## ✨ Features

- 🚓 **Supervisor Investigation** — Track residents, gather information, and call the police before criminals get away.
- 🕵️ **Hidden Snitch** — Blend into the suspect group while secretly reporting suspicious behavior.
- 🎭 **Eleven Suspect Roles** — Play distinctive roles such as Thief, Gambler, Pyromaniac, Drunk, or Prankster.
- 🎙️ **Proximity Voice Chat** — Conversations depend on who is close enough to hear what you say.
- 🗳️ **Emergency Meetings** — Gather residents, debate suspicious behavior, and vote on who may be the Snitch.
- 🧠 **Sanity And Urges** — Satisfy role-specific urges while keeping your criminal habits hidden.
- 🤝 **Online And LAN Multiplayer** — Play competitive and cooperative social-deduction matches with friends.
- 🏚️ **Interactive Trailer Park** — Search trailers, lots, community spaces, and the local saloon for opportunities and evidence.
